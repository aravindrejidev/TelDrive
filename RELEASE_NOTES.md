## What's new

- **In-app updates.** TeleDrive now checks for new releases when you open it and shows what changed. Tap **Update now** to download, verify and install it without leaving the app.
- **Settings, reorganised.** Settings is now a list of sections, each with its own page: Appearance, Library, Uploads, Sync, Storage and data, Telegram connection and Updates.
- **More to choose from.** Five accent colours, haptic feedback on or off, a default sort order and start tab, silent uploads, and syncing only on Wi-Fi, only while charging or only when the battery is not low.
- **An About page** with the version and build, the developer, and links to the source code and release history.
- **A new look.** A soft, premium redesign with light and dark themes, and a brand new app icon and start-up screen.
- **Search and sort** your files, with an upload queue and a Retry button for failed uploads.

## Fixes

- Files you remove are no longer uploaded again by Sync.
- Photos keep their original quality instead of being compressed.
- Large syncs wait out Telegram's rate limit instead of failing.
- Files that are still being copied are skipped until they finish.
