## What's new

- **In-app updates.** TeleDrive now checks for new releases when you open it and shows what changed. Tap **Update now** to download, verify and install it without leaving the app.
- **A new look.** A soft, premium redesign with light and dark themes.
- **Search and sort** your files, with an upload queue and a Retry button for failed uploads.
- **Open in Telegram** for files too large for a bot to download.

## Fixes

- Files you remove are no longer uploaded again by Sync.
- Photos keep their original quality instead of being compressed.
- Large syncs wait out Telegram's rate limit instead of failing.
- Files that are still being copied are skipped until they finish.
